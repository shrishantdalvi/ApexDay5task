# Apex Lark – Day 05: Advanced Excel Business Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on performing advanced business analysis using Microsoft Excel and a cleaned Superstore dataset. The objective was to analyze sales, profit, customer contributions, product performance and regional trends using Excel functions and reporting techniques.

## Objectives
- Perform lookup operations across multiple worksheets.
- Apply logical functions to classify sales and profit performance.
- Calculate important business KPIs.
- Identify top-performing customers and products.
- Generate meaningful business insights from the dataset.

## Worksheets Created

| Worksheet | Description |
|---|---|
| Sales Data | Organized transaction-level sales and profit data. |
| Customer Data | Customer details, total sales and customer rankings. |
| Product Data | Product details, categories, calculated unit prices and sales rankings. |
| Final Report | Combined analysis, lookup results, logical classifications, KPIs and business summaries. |

## Key Business Insights

1. **Highest Revenue Region:** West
2. **Highest Sales Customer:** Sean Miller
3. **Most Profitable Category:** Office Supplies

### Business Problems Identified
- Most records contribute low-to-medium levels of sales and profit.
- Profit contribution is comparatively concentrated in the West Region.
- East and Central regions generate comparatively lower profit.

## Conclusion
This project provided practical experience in advanced Excel functions, multi-worksheet analysis, business KPI reporting and identifying actionable insights from sales data.

The analysis helped understand customer contributions, product category profitability and regional sales performance.
